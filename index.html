Mission Briefing

How Rally Week Works

Before launch day, here's the rundown every crew member needs.

01

Squads of 20

Sign up solo or with friends and get placed on a team of 20. Every squad is folded into one of the larger color teams for the week.

02

Team Leaders

Each squad has 3 team leaders (sophomores and up) who keep everyone safe, on schedule, and checked in at every event.

03

Points & Titles

Squads rack up points all week by showing up and competing. Top teams advance to Saturday's Maker's Comp for a final shot at the crown.

04

Clubs & Crews

Dozens of student clubs, Greek chapters, and local Troy businesses run stations, stages, and competitions throughout the week.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up with a group?

Not at all — sign up solo and you'll be placed on a squad of 20 alongside other students. You can also register together as an existing group of friends.

Is Rally Week only for first-years?

No. Rally Week is open to all RPI students. Sophomores and up are especially encouraged to sign up as team leaders.

What should I bring to Color Wars?

Clothes you don't mind getting soaked and dyed, closed-toe shoes, and a water bottle. More details go out to registered squads before 9/15.

How do teams win?

Squads earn points throughout the week for attendance and competition results. The top 20 squads advance to Saturday's Maker's Comp for a final shot at the overall Rally Week title.

How can my club or business get involved?

Use the "Get Involved" section above and select the option that fits — student club, Greek chapter, or local business — and our team will follow up with details.
