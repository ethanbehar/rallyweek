Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up with a group?

Not at all. You can either sign up solo or with one friend, and you'll be placed on a team of 20 alongside other students.

Is Rally Week only for first-years?

Rally Week is open to all RPI students. Sophomores and up are especially encouraged to sign up as team leaders.

What should I bring?

There are events every day during the week. Just show up with a good spirit!

How do teams win?

Teams earn points throughout the week by attending events and participating in challenges. Top teams earn prizes and awards at the Finale Ceremony.

How can I volunteer?

Head to the Get Involved section and sign up to join the volunteer team.

What if I have a conflict or am in a club?

If you can't make it to any events, that's totally fine - though your team may lose some points. If you're volunteering or part of an involved club, you'll be exempt from losing points!
